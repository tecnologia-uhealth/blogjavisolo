The problem

Only 10% of Mexicans have major medical insurance.

Thirty-nine cents of every peso spent on health in Mexico comes straight out of a family's pocket — the second highest out-of-pocket burden in the OECD, where the average is 19%. Mexico spends USD 1,588 per person per year on health against an OECD average of 5,967, and has 2.7 physicians per thousand people against 3.9.

That gap will not close in a decade, no matter the budget. And while it stays open, people get sick anyway.
